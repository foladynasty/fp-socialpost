==========================================
VERCEL DEPLOYMENT INSTRUCTIONS
==========================================

Run these commands in your terminal:

1. Login to Vercel:
   vercel login

2. Deploy to preview:
   vercel

3. Set environment variables (when prompted or manually):
   - VITE_SUPABASE_URL
   - VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY

4. Deploy to production:
   vercel --prod

==========================================
Your app will be live at:
https://fp-socialpost-xxx.vercel.app
